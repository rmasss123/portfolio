01 / ORIGIN

I grew up
in patterns.

subject: tiwari_s · status: shipping

It started with a maths olympiad in 2019. I was 15, and I realized that the most interesting thing about numbers wasn't solving them — it was what they revealed about the world underneath. That instinct led me to NIT Bhopal, a B.Tech in Mathematics & Data Science, and eventually to the question I keep coming back to: what is this messy human data actually trying to say?

At Leap in Bengaluru, I found the answer in conversations. Every day, hundreds of counselors talk to students about studying abroad — and almost none of those conversations were being heard at scale. I built the systems that changed that. LLM pipelines that score calls, detect risk, translate across four languages, and surface the students who need help before they disappear.

Outside work, I build things I'm curious about — an arena where two AIs debate, a tool that lets you talk to your 80-year-old self. I compete, lead teams, and sometimes just sit with a hard problem until it opens up.

03 / EXPERIMENTS

Things I built
because I wondered.

P·01

AI Debate Arena

I kept wondering: what happens when two AIs argue? So I built the arena. Two AI personas clash on any topic in real-time over SSE streams. The crowd votes. The results are stored. It's part experiment, part spectacle — and it reveals a lot about how language models reason under pressure.

P·02

Future Self

Built for myself, honestly. When I'm stuck on a hard decision, I want perspective — not advice from someone my age, but from the versions of me that have already lived through it. Describe your dilemma. Three future yous respond simultaneously — you at +5 years, +20 years, and at 80. Then you talk to each one.

P·03

Sentiment Analysis × Metaheuristics

My deep learning research project — a BiLSTM + Conv1D + Attention model trained on 2M+ samples. What made it different: instead of manual hyperparameter tuning, I used Genetic Algorithm and Grey Wolf Optimization to evolve the best config. Hit 95% on Amazon Food Reviews, 85% on Sentiment140.

TensorFlowBiLSTMGA · GWO

P·04

Human Activity Recognition

A systematic benchmark — I wanted to understand how different CNN architectures actually compare in practice, not just in papers. Ran VGG, ResNet, GoogLeNet, DenseNet, and MobileNetV2 on 12,000+ images across 15 activity classes with consistent transfer learning and augmentation. Learned more from the gaps than the results.

CNNTransfer learningKeras

P·05

Heart Disease Prediction

One of my earliest ML projects — and still one of the most grounding ones. Clinical features: age, blood pressure, cholesterol, ECG readings. The question: can a model flag heart disease risk before symptoms do? Full EDA, logistic regression, evaluation across precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC. Medicine taught me to care about false negatives.
